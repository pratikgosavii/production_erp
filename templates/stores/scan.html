How this works

The whole point is that nothing gets written on paper twice.
  1. A QR label was generated and pasted on the physical sheet when the lot arrived.
  2. Scan that label here, or with any phone camera — the QR carries a full URL.
  3. The sheet's detail form opens with whatever is already known about it.
  4. Fill in or update material, grade, supplier, rack and status, then save.
  5. The sheet is live stock — visible in Material Inventory and reservable by Design.
