These QRs encode a full scan URL, not a bare sheet number. The old register printed just the integer, so pointing a phone camera at a label did nothing. Each label below encodes https://erp.local/stores/sheet/SHT-0813/ — any stock phone camera opens the sheet form straight away, no app needed.
